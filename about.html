What it is

NexGen BBPro is a league-first baseball sim where strategy and realism meet. You are not just managing a lineup, you are steering a living league with draft classes, injuries, awards, history, and every decision that shapes a franchise.

Why it feels real

The simulation engine is built to keep long-run stats grounded, while still delivering dramatic game-to-game swings. Pitcher usage, contact quality, and defensive outcomes all matter, so smart roster construction pays off over a full season.
