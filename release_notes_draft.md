## 7.1.0
# 7.1 Release Notes — CPU League Realism (Sprint 2)
## CPU roster management
- **Deadline-aware CPU trading:** contenders buy (chase veterans), rebuilders
- **CPU-to-CPU trades:** CPU teams now trade with each other, not just with you
- **In-season callups + September expansion:** CPU teams promote AAA prospects
## On-field realism (physics engine)
- Platoon-aware lineups vs LHP/RHP and a modern slot-weighted batting order.
- Pitch-count-based reliever rest, closers used in tied 9th innings/extras, and
- Times-through-order penalty (batters improve on later looks), pinch-hitters
- New KPI gates hold league AVG/OBP/SLG, K%/BB%, home-run and usage
## Fixes
- League history and the leagues list endpoints fixed; assorted stability and

- Finance: new Payroll vs Luxury Threshold card on the Finance page - visual floor/payroll/threshold meter, headroom, estimated tax/floor fee, and Opening Day solvency status, using the same math settlement applies.
- Free Agency: offer dialog now previews the signing's impact on your books - payroll before/after vs the luxury threshold, estimated tax, cash remaining after the signing bonus (with debt-accrual warning), and Opening Day solvency risk.
- API: added GET /teams/{team_id}/finance/payroll-context and a payroll_impact block on the free-agent evaluate-offer response.
- Contracts: salary column now shows compact values with total commitment (e.g. 8.5M/yr, 25.5M over 3 yrs), service time reads as years and days, and the Expiring / Arb-eligible / options / Non-gtd badges explain themselves on hover.
- Finance settings: each module level dropdown (league creation + commissioner) now explains what the selected level actually changes.
- Finance Queue: rows now show player names, plain-language decisions, and salary movement instead of raw ids/tokens, and Apply Approved shows a confirmation summary of exactly what will be committed.
- Help: tutorials and manuals updated for the new finance UX - Finance Hub tutorial covers the Payroll vs Luxury Threshold card and the Contracts tracker, Free Agency tutorial covers the live signing-impact panel, admin tutorial covers the humanized Finance Queue, and the Finance System Manual section 8 now describes hybrid enforcement instead of the retired warn/block modes.
- UI fixes: the standings button in the status ribbon and the Season page's regenerate-schedule link now navigate correctly; commissioner-only pages consistently redirect owners; deleting a league uses the in-app confirm dialog; the command palette now lists every page (filtered by role) instead of a stale hand-kept list.
- Performance: team logos and player avatars are now fetched once and shared across the whole session instead of once per table row; initial page load is ~40 percent smaller (Firebase now loads only when cloud sign-in is used); server auth no longer blocks concurrent requests (membership lookups are cached).
- Simulation: breaking and offspeed pitches now arrive slower than fastballs (per-type velocity), and the KPI harness gates many more realism metrics (slash line, contact quality, first-pitch strikes, ISO, batted-ball mix).
- Performance: season simulation is another ~2.6x faster (240-game benchmark 23.6s to ~9s) via day-batched stats/recovery persistence, shared file caches, and engine inner-loop optimizations - with byte-identical simulation results verified at every step.
- Performance: faster page loads - league contract tracker no longer rescans roster files per row, player profiles read the finance ledger once instead of twice, cloud saves sync only the active league's files, and big tables (players, contracts, stats, schedule) use virtualized rendering.
- Fix: schedule rows now store boxscore file paths instead of embedding full HTML into schedule.csv - fixes broken boxscore links from the Schedule page and stops the schedule file from growing by megabytes over a season.
- Pitching (7.38.0): a pitcher's declared role (SP/RP) now outranks the endurance guess. Leagues whose pitchers sit just under the old endurance cutoff had no recognised starters at all (alpha-test: 0 of 460; now 252).
- Leaders (7.38.1): league leaderboards now show each player's team.
- Trades (7.39.0): you can counter any offer, including one from another owner, not just CPU offers. The counter dialog uses searchable roster pickers and the live acceptance meter instead of typed player ids.
- Teams (7.39.1): every team page has a full roster browser - Active, AAA, LOW and injured-list tabs with position, age, bats/throws and overall - so you can scout a trade partner's whole organisation.
- Pitching (7.40.0): starters are now rested and budgeted as starters, using your own SP1-SP5 staff assignments. Every pitcher had been stored with a stale "RP" role, which gave starters reliever rest. Starter selection can also be logged for diagnosis (NEXGEN_ROTATION_DIAGNOSTICS).
- Pitching (7.41.0): the game now starts the pitcher the rotation actually chose. The engine had been re-picking a starter from a counter that reset on every sim run, so each weekly run replayed SP1, SP2, SP3, SP4, SP5, SP1, SP2 and the top two starters got roughly double the starts of the other three.
- Pitching (7.42.0): when one of your starters is on the injured list, his rotation spot goes to a pitcher who can actually start - your long man before your closer, never the reverse - and a poor fill no longer sticks for the rest of the season.
- Team stats (7.43.0): Team Totals are now the sum of the players currently on your roster, since season stats travel with a player when he is traded. Your club's W-L record is shown separately and is unchanged. Averages and ERA are recomputed from their components rather than averaged.
- Draft (7.44.0): drafts with human owners now work. "Advance to next owner" runs the CPU teams and stops when an owner is up, and every advance button now stops at owners. An optional pick clock lets the CPU pick for an owner whose time runs out, with an on-the-clock post to Discord and a live countdown on the draft page.
- Fix (7.44.0): trade offers sent to another owner were being answered automatically by the CPU. Team ownership is now read from league membership, not the empty owner column in teams.csv.
- Draft (7.44.1): the draft only runs by itself once a pick clock is set. At 0 it never advances on its own; the commissioner drives it.
- Draft (7.44.2): the commissioner can make or auto-pick the selection for whichever team is on the clock ("Pick for TEAM"), so an owner who isn't responding can't stall the draft.
- Fix (7.44.3): an amateur draft in progress now keeps the season paused. Previously, once a league had made even one pick, the next scheduled run resumed the regular season and simulated straight through the draft.
- Draft (7.44.4): new **My picks** card on the Draft page (Now and History tabs) lists every player your team has drafted with a position strip (C, 1B, 2B, 3B, SS, LF, CF, RF, P) counting what you have taken, so empty positions stand out before your next pick. A team picker lets you view any team's picks. Draft tutorial updated, including the 7.44 pick clock, Advance to next owner, and commissioner Pick for TEAM.
- Draft (7.44.5): the commissioner's **Auto-finish draft** works again. It makes every remaining pick, owners included, after a confirmation listing whose picks will be made; since 7.44.0 it had stopped at the first owner and made no picks. It is now commissioner-only, and the server refuses an owner's request to auto-pick for other owners.
- Player profiles (7.44.6): the strikeout column (K) now shows for every hitter and pitcher; it read a field the simulation never writes, so it was blank for everyone. Pitchers' win percentage and opponents' batting average are now filled in, the always-blank DERA column is replaced by FIP, ERA/FIP/WHIP read to two decimals, and innings show in baseball notation (70.1, not 70.333).
- Simulation (7.44.6): pinch runners are now used only for the tying or go-ahead run. Bench speedsters had been sent in for almost any slow runner from the 7th inning on, even with the team trailing by eight; over 20 simulated days bench appearances fell from 2.16 to 0.94 per team-game, and specialists who were used in about 85% of games now appear in 15-45% of them. Those extra pinch runners had been worth about 0.08 runs a game, so the overall run environment is nudged back up to compensate (offense_scale 1.015 to 1.0175); league scoring stays where it was and the calibration gates pass on both reference seeds.
- Simulation (7.45.0): **power now hits for power.** The Power rating had almost no effect on home runs while Contact drove them (HR rate vs Power r = -0.06, vs Contact +0.77 on alpha-test), so high-average, ordinary-power hitters posted sluggers' numbers. Contact now decides whether and how squarely the ball is met; Power decides how hard it is hit, with a curve that leaves average hitters average and lets genuine sluggers separate. League scoring and home-run totals are unchanged; who hits the home runs is not. On a 30-day sim of alpha-test, HR vs Power went from +0.01 to +0.45 and the home-run leaders went from contact hitters with power ratings of 48-52 to sluggers rated 60-63. The calibration harness now gates that ratings drive the outcomes they name.
- Fix (7.45.1): a file-permission helper written for Windows (`os.chmod(path, stat.S_IWRITE)`) stripped read and execute access from every league data directory on Linux, so nothing inside could be opened. Production was unaffected only because Cloud Run runs as root; it is what crashed the calibration check on GitHub. It now adds owner-write and leaves the rest of the mode alone. The calibration CI check also now runs at all: it had failed every one of its 64 runs since January, first on committed scratch symlinks under tmp/, then on a stale requirements.txt name, then on this.
- Fix (7.45.2): drafted players no longer disappear from rosters. A shared ownership registry for drafted players was kept once per server process instead of once per league, and draft ids repeat across leagues (D20260013 exists in every 2026 draft), so another league's draft decided who owned alpha-test's picks and they were silently dropped on roster load. Each league now has its own registry, the roster files are the authority on who a player plays for, and entries naming teams from other leagues are purged.
- Rosters (7.45.3): the CPU never trims an owner's team. When a deadline passes, an owner's roster is only fixed where it is illegal ("fill gaps"); nobody is released unless the owner runs Auto-reassign themselves. And no player from this year's draft class is released just for room on any team: auto-assign releases the weakest non-draftee instead, or keeps the pick and leaves the team over the limit.
- Trades (7.45.4): trading a minor leaguer now takes him off his old team. The trade screen offers AAA, Low-A and injured players, but the trade only removed the player from the old team's active roster, so he ended up listed by both clubs (HOU's Erich Zenk was on HOU's and BAL's AAA rosters for a month after HOU traded him). For a traded draft pick it was worse: the next roster reload dropped him from his new team and the trade was silently undone. Draft picks in a trade are also now transferred before any roster changes, so a rejected pick cannot leave a half-applied trade behind, and the transaction log records the level the player actually left.
