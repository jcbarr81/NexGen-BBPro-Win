NexGen-BBPro Finance System Manual

This manual explains the complete finance system: modules, settings, terms, timing, queue flow, and the exact owner/commissioner progression through a season.

What changed in 7.0 (read first). Several behaviors below describe the older model and have been superseded:
  • Enforcement is now On or Off (the old warn / block modes are gone; old configs map to On). When On, the rules are hybrid: during the season you may exceed the luxury threshold but you pay the luxury tax (and a floor fee for underspending) — nothing is blocked mid‑season. The one hard gate is Opening Day, where a team must be solvent (projected debt within the cap) to start the season.
  • Money actually moves. Draft‑pick slot bonuses and free‑agent signing bonuses debit team cash (accruing debt if short); declined‑option buyouts hit cash too.
  • Qualifying offers & compensation picks. Eligible departing free agents can receive a one‑year qualifying offer (owner‑controlled for human teams via the Free Agency page). A declined QO whose player signs elsewhere yields a compensation draft pick for the former team and a forfeited pick for the signing team.
  • Owner reminders. A phase‑aware finance to‑do on the Season page surfaces budget, luxury‑threshold/cash, arbitration, and qualifying‑offer actions for your team.
Module enable/disable, presets (Off / Simple / Standard / MLB‑Like / Custom), contracts, arbitration, the revenue model, and the luxury‑tax bands all work as described below.

1. System Overview

The finance system is league-scoped and configurable. It can run in a light mode or deep mode, and it supports owner workflows plus commissioner oversight.

  • Finance can be fully disabled or enabled per league.
  • Each finance module can run at different levels.
  • Payroll policy is hybrid: in-season violations settle economically (luxury tax, floor fee); only Opening Day insolvency blocks.
  • Owner and commissioner modes have different queue approval behavior.

4. Presets, Enforcement Modes, and AI Tuning

Presets

  • off: finance disabled and modules effectively off.
  • simple: lighter economics with reduced rule strictness.
  • standard: balanced default with richer owner and GM finance.
  • mlb_like: strictest profile with stronger payroll enforcement behavior.
  • custom: manually tuned module mix.

Enforcement Meaning (On / Off hybrid)

  • off: policy violations reported minimally and not enforced.
  • on: MLB-style hybrid — during the season you may exceed the luxury threshold (you pay the tax at settlement; underspending draws a floor fee) and nothing is blocked mid-season. The one hard gate is Opening Day: a team must be solvent (projected debt within its cap) to start the season.
  • Legacy warn/block values from older configs are automatically upgraded to on.

AI Tuning

Finance AI uses configurable thresholds (talent/performance/salary pressure) to steer arbitration and free-agent recommendation behavior.

5. The Finance Page (Layout & Budgets)

The Finance page (My Team → Finance) is a single scrolling page — there are no separate "Owner Ops" / "GM Ops" tabs. Top to bottom it shows:

  • Headline stats: cash on hand, debt, and projected net.
  • Payroll vs Luxury Threshold (when payroll rules are on): headroom, estimated tax/floor fee, and Opening-Day solvency — the same math settlement charges.
  • Payroll alerts: proactive warnings (low cash, high debt, negative projected net).
  • Revenue and Expenses: actual vs projection by category.
  • Budgets — editable (below).
  • Arbitration — when gm_arbitration is on (see section 6).
  • Transactions: the finance ledger.

Editing Budgets

  • The Budgets card is editable when finance is enabled AND the owner_budgets module is not off (Simple preset and up).
  • Click Edit, set each category — training, scouting, development, facilities — and click Save budgets. The projected figure next to each is your revenue-based ceiling.
  • On presets where owner_budgets is off, the card is read-only.
  • Budget changes feed multiple gameplay systems (see section 9).
Good workflow: set budgets before preseason operations and re-check after major roster cost changes.

6. Owner Actions: Contracts, Arbitration, Free Agency

Arbitration (on the Finance page)

  • When gm_arbitration is on (Standard and MLB-Like presets — it is OFF in Simple), an Arbitration panel lists your arbitration-eligible players with current vs projected salary.
  • For each player you choose Offer raise (agree to the projected raise), Hold, or Non-tender (release to free agency). These are direct owner decisions — there is no separate "recommend, then commissioner-review" step for the owner.
  • Players become arbitration-eligible only after roughly three seasons of service, so a brand-new league shows an empty panel.

Contract actions (on the Player Profile page)

  • Extend: negotiate an extension from a player's profile (Contract card) when the contracts module is on.
  • Options and Renew: on the advanced contracts level (Standard / MLB-Like), the Contract card gains an Owner actions section for players you own — Exercise or Decline a pending option (a declined option's buyout hits your cash), and Renew a pre-arbitration player's salary for next year (floored at the league minimum).

Free agency & qualifying offers

  • Sign free agents and tender/decline qualifying offers from the Free Agency page (see sections 8 and 11).
All owner finance actions (extend, sign, qualifying offer, budgets, arbitration, option, renew) are owner-or-commissioner only — enforced server-side.

7. GM Finance Queue and Commissioner Review Flow

Single-Player Leagues

  • Recommended queue decisions are auto-approved locally.
  • Owners can iterate quickly with less admin gating.

Multi-Owner Leagues

  • Queue decisions enter commissioner review states.
  • Commissioner approves/rejects from admin queue tools.
  • Approved decisions are applied by queue-processing workflow.
Queue status values matter: pending, approved-not-applied, applied. Always verify final applied state.

8. Payroll Policy Checks (Hybrid Enforcement)

What is evaluated

  • Projected payroll after signings, contract updates, and trade deltas.
  • Threshold overages, floor shortfalls, and debt-cap pressure.
  • Rule level behavior based on current preset/module configuration.

Outcomes

  • Pass: no violations found.
  • Over threshold / under floor: the action is allowed in-season; the overage settles as a luxury tax (MLB-Like: tiered CBT; Basic: flat overage fee) and a shortfall as a floor fee. Violations are reported to notifications/UI so owners can react.
  • Blocked (Opening Day only): the single hard gate — a team whose projected debt exceeds its cap cannot start the season until it is solvent again.

The system formats policy messages with projected values and threshold deltas to explain the decision. Owners can see the same numbers up front: the Payroll vs Luxury Threshold card on the Finance page (headroom, estimated tax/fee, Opening Day solvency) and the live "Your team's books" panel in the Free Agency offer dialog preview exactly what settlement would charge.

9. Budget Effects on Development and Scouting

Training/Development/Facilities

  • Budget levels influence training-camp intensity multipliers.
  • Development budgets also influence offseason aging/development outcomes.
  • Facilities contributes to overall training camp support strength.

Scouting

  • Scouting budget affects scouting confidence profile.
  • Lower scouting support increases displayed rating uncertainty.
  • Higher scouting support tightens confidence and lowers estimated error.
  • Your scouting budget (set on the Finance page's Budgets card) trades credit spend for faster confidence improvement.
  • League scouting intensity (off / low / normal / high) is a league-level default the commissioner sets at league creation and from Admin → Commissioner — it is not a per-owner control.
  • Commissioners tune global scouting pace from Admin → Commissioner → Scouting (base credits, finance-off multiplier, decay/passive gain, bank cap, auto-spend cap).

11. Offseason Pipeline and Stage-by-Stage Progression

Pipeline Core

  1. Generate season-end finance snapshot.
  2. Apply arbitration updates where enabled.
  3. Reset/prepare team financial year state.
  4. Apply payroll rule accounting effects.

Checklist Stages

  1. run_pipeline
  2. contracts_review
  3. arbitration_review
  4. gm_finance_review
  5. budgets_review
  6. free_agency_kickoff
  7. finalize

Stage order is enforced. The next required stage must be completed before later stages can be marked done.

12. Alerts, Risk Signals, and What to Do Next

Common Alert Types

  • Cash/debt risk pressure.
  • Payroll threshold or floor pressure.
  • GM queue backlog or unapplied approved decisions.
  • Offseason checklist not finalized before progression.
  • League Command Center finance-risk cards aggregating critical/warning items with next-step actions.

Typical Response Pattern

  1. Open the relevant page (Finance, Free Agency, a player's profile, the League Command Center, or the Admin finance queue).
  2. Resolve blocked/pending actions first.
  3. Re-run overview/refresh and validate alerts clear or downgrade.

13. Finance Data Files and Persistence

Finance data is league-scoped. Common files include:

  • league_financial_settings.json
  • team_financials.json
  • contracts.json
  • financial_transactions.csv
  • gm_finance_decisions.json

14. Recommended Finance Playbook

In-Season (Owner)

  1. Check the Finance page's monthly projection and debt trend.
  2. Handle any arbitration decisions and contract options/renewals as they come up.
  3. Validate payroll warnings before major signings or trades.

In-Season (Commissioner)

  1. Monitor queue pending volume and resolve approval backlog.
  2. Watch threshold/floor alert summaries and communicate policy expectations.
  3. Verify no unresolved finance blockers before major simulation jumps.

Offseason

  1. Run pipeline once and validate snapshot output.
  2. Complete checklist stages in order.
  3. Ensure GM queue is fully resolved/applied before finalization.
  4. Confirm budgets and kick off free agency cleanly.

15. Troubleshooting and Validation

Actions disabled or hidden on the Finance page

Check the module level and the global finance-enabled state. When a module is off its actions are hidden (e.g. the Arbitration panel is hidden unless gm_arbitration is on, and the Budgets card is read-only unless owner_budgets is on). Owner actions also require that you own the team.

Queue action did not apply

Check review status and whether commissioner approval is still pending.

Policy blocked a signing/trade

Review policy message details: projected payroll/debt and threshold overage amount.

Projection seems stale

Use refresh, verify active league, and ensure phase/cadence events have run.
