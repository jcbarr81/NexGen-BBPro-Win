1. Startup and League Selection

Start Flow

  • Launch the app and select Start Game.
  • Choose Load Existing League or Create New League.
  • Log in with your assigned account role (owner or admin).

Role Behavior

  • Owner: team operations, roster control, finance hub, and owner tools.
  • Admin: league-wide operations, policy settings, approvals, and lifecycle actions.
The active league context drives all data reads/writes. Verify the league badge before making changes.

3. Roster Management and Player Movement

Roster Levels

  • ACT: active major-league roster.
  • AAA: upper reserve level for ready replacements.
  • LOW: development or overflow depth level.

Primary Movement Paths

  • Owner Tools -> Reassign Players for direct promotions/demotions.
  • Injury actions can auto-open active roster spots and prompt replacements.
  • When team auto-reassign is enabled, injury/trade/signing updates can auto-balance ACT/AAA/LOW after the move.
After major moves, revisit depth chart and lineups so simulation logic uses the new personnel correctly.

4. Lineups, Pitching Staff, and Depth Chart

Lineup Editor

  • Maintain both vs RHP and vs LHP lineups.
  • Use Auto-Fill as a baseline, then make strategic manual edits.
  • Save before closing; saved files are used immediately by simulation.

Pitching Staff

  • Assign rotation order and bullpen roles (CL, SU, MR, LR, etc.).
  • Watch fatigue and recovery context when setting usage expectations.

Depth Chart

  • Set positional priority replacements to automate injury and promotion fallback.
  • Use drag-and-drop order and save chart updates.

5. Injury Center and Recovery Flow

Core Actions

  • Place on DL for fixed minimum list usage.
  • Place on IR for long-term storage until manual reactivation.
  • Recover to Destination to return a player when eligibility is met.
  • Promote Best Replacement to keep active roster staffed.

Timing Notes

  • DL minimums are enforced before activation is permitted.
  • Recovery and movement actions are logged for audit and visibility.

6. Trades and Transactions

Trade Center

  • Build offers with players and, if allowed, draft-pick assets.
  • League trade settings control approval mode and future-pick limits.
  • If commissioner approval is enabled, owner acceptance does not execute immediately.

Roster & Lineup Edits

  • Roster, lineup, pitching, and depth-chart changes apply directly to the shared league data through the web app — no export or file exchange.
  • The commissioner can reverse a committed trade if it is lopsided.

Transaction Visibility

  • Use transaction logs and news feed entries for verification.
  • Completed actions should always be validated against roster state.

7. Owner Finance Hub

Owner Ops Tab

  • Current cash/debt state and projected monthly revenue/expense view.
  • Budget targets for training, scouting, development, and facilities.
  • Scouting Controls with intensity selection and confidence/error-band status.
  • Ledger history for posted finance activity.

GM/Coach Ops Tab

  • Module status and effective level visibility.
  • Contracts and payroll summary with action buttons.
  • Arbitration/free-agency queue guidance and recommended actions.
  • Workflow panel showing what should happen next by phase.

For complete finance mechanics, open the dedicated Finance System Manual from the Tutorials menu.

8. Schedule, League Hub, and History

  • Team Schedule: team-specific upcoming games and results.
  • League schedule: full slate context and seasonal travel rhythm.
  • Standings: division and league race tracking.
  • League Command Center: unified triage for injuries, approvals, roster conflicts, deadlines, and finance-risk alerts.
  • Leaders/Records: narrative and performance milestones.
  • League History: past champions and historical context.

9. Training, Draft, and Free Agency

Training Focus

  • Player-level, team-level, and league-level focus sources are supported.
  • Bulk apply options exist in roster table workflows.
  • Training history and impacts can be reviewed after training events.

Amateur Draft

  • Draft pool viewing and draft console become active at draft timing gates.
  • Manual picks, auto pick, and auto draft all are supported.
  • Committed draftees are written to player data and assigned to development rosters.

Free Agency

  • Browse unsigned player pool, evaluate fit, and execute signings.
  • Finance and payroll policy can warn or block actions by league configuration.

11. Season Progression and Operational Timing

Recommended Order

  1. Preseason setup: lineups, pitching, depth chart, budgets/policies.
  2. Regular season simulation in controlled increments.
  3. Draft handling at the draft window.
  4. Playoff transition and postseason operations.
  5. Offseason finance workflow and preparation for next year.
In multi-owner leagues, communicate major phase changes before running league-wide actions.

12. Reports, Exports, and Asset Utilities

  • Export reports in HTML (default) or CSV when you need shareable standings, stats, and analytics bundles.
  • Use Export Almanac (HTML) to build a multi-page league history site with a landing page for seasons, teams, players, awards, postseason, leaders, transactions, finance, and records.
  • Generate team logos and player avatars from admin utilities.
  • Use snapshot exports before destructive or high-impact operations.

13. Practical Checklists

Owner Daily/Weekly Checklist

  1. Review injuries and roster eligibility.
  2. Validate lineups and pitching roles after moves.
  3. Check finance guidance and pending queues.
  4. Review standings and upcoming schedule blocks.

Commissioner Checklist Before Major Sim Runs

  1. Confirm active league and phase.
  2. Resolve pending trade and change-request items.
  3. Check finance queue/alerts if enabled.
  4. Run exports/snapshots for rollback safety. For public history or release-ready archives, include an Almanac export and open its landing page once to confirm the bundle is browsable.

14. FAQ and Troubleshooting

Why did a move not execute?

Check roster limits, policy settings (trades/payroll), and commissioner approval requirements.

Why is a player not eligible to return from DL?

DL minimum service time may not be met yet. Check injury center timing fields.

Why are finance buttons disabled?

Financial system or specific modules may be set to off in league settings.

How do I find exact terminology quickly?

Use the manual search field and terms like Owner Ops, GM queue, trade approval, or offseason checklist.
